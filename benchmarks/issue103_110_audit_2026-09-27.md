## #110: the broad acceptance-rate premise was false

The September 9 metadata claimed 40 images spanning all 21 strata. The retained
`acc_grid.tsv` instead names exactly general-photo images **1000 through 1039**.
Each has seven encode rows: lossless u8/u16/derived-f32 at e8/e9 and lossy u16
at e9. The historical harness globally sorted paths and took the first 40
usable PNGs; it did not stratify selection.

Every one of the 1,200 committed greedy-call records matches `acc_grid.log`
in order on symbols, bit decrease, threshold, acceptance and match count.
Thus 160 accepted / 1,040 rejected and 877 below 0.01x threshold remain valid
counts **for those calls**, not across all content classes. Calls are also
not independent images; one encode can invoke the matcher more than once.
The historical rows have no source hashes or encoded artifacts, so this
audit cannot verify their source pixels or bitstream identity.

[Recovered source list](lz77_acceptance_sources_2026-09-27.tsv) and
[retained-file hashes](issue103_110_audit_2026-09-27.meta.json) bind this
correction. Do not use the 86.7% rejection rate to justify bypassing LZ77
on screenshots, line art, documents or native HDR inputs.

The bucketed matcher is already implemented and archived, contrary to the
issue's last comment. All **72** retained artifacts in its six-arm screen
still match recorded lengths and SHA256s. All five bucket capacities produce
29,702 bytes on terminal's derived-f32 crop versus 22,072 for the chain.
[The rejection report](lz77_bucket_screen_2026-09-24.md) remains supported.
This rejects unconditional adoption of that candidate; its block-ordered
single observations do not establish a timing result. The archive
`abandoned/issue110-bucket-default` remains available at `deac9c61e897`.

## The experimental keep-best path is not a complete size decision

Source trace in `jxl-encoder/src/entropy_coding/lz77.rs`:

- `lz77_beats_plain_on_real_cost` writes ANS metadata and tokens, but never
  calls `write_lz77_header`. `encode_ans::write_entropy_code_ans` explicitly
  leaves that header to its caller. The comparator omits a real serialized
  difference between plain and LZ77 streams.
- It builds each stream with `total_pixel_hint: None`. Production's whole-image
  modular path builds combined tokens with `Some(total_pixels)` in `section.rs`.
  Per-stream costs need not choose the smallest complete output.
- `apply_lz77_optimal` uses the greedy result to seed its cost model, then
  returns its DP result without comparing the final coded size against either
  plain or greedy. A greedy-stage verdict is not a verdict on that final stream.
- Huffman and cost-write failure branches return `true`; they do not establish
  a smaller candidate. The caller's positive-saving filter is weaker than the
  ordinary acceptance threshold.

These are source-proven limitations of the opt-in experiment, not new measured
regression sizes. Default keep-best remains off. The prior real-graphics
keep-best results cannot justify a no-regression guarantee.

The clean next experiments are (1) verify complete candidate-cost accounting
at the production coding boundary, including final Optimal output and original
clustering context, and (2) measure shorter chains against the **current
early-out-enabled baseline**, retaining artifacts and identifying accepted
streams on an explicit content-stratified input manifest. The old chain timing
does not establish the incremental win after early-out shipped. Neither route
requires changing defaults or widening the public strategy API first.

## Upstream retention and the keep-best follow-up

Live source check on September 27: libjxl main is
`b87738951c1254cd8cccaa6d47712ba735da56d8`.
[`HistogramParams::ForModular`](https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl/blob/b87738951c1254cd8cccaa6d47712ba735da56d8/lib/jxl/enc_ans.cc#L1345)
still selects `kLZ77b3w3f` in its intermediate high-effort branch.
[`ApplyLZ77`](https://github.com/libjxl/libjxl/blob/b87738951c1254cd8cccaa6d47712ba735da56d8/lib/jxl/enc_lz77.cc#L779)
dispatches that choice to the three-entry bucket matcher without per-match
cost rejection. Its final acceptance remains the estimated
`bit_decrease > total_symbols * 0.2 + 16` test. This confirms retention on
main, not release inclusion or parity of our archived port. Our reference
target remains v0.12.

A complete keep-best experiment should retain the current production result
as an incumbent, compare complete affected coding units under the actual
production histogram settings (including headers and final Optimal tokens),
and emit the selected candidate's already-built state. Comparing only bucket
versus plain would not protect against losing to the existing chain result.
The terminal derived-f32 regression and the frymire derived-f32 improvement
provide opposite selection cases; both need single/multi-group, exact-pixel
checks through the primary Rust decoder and djxl. This is a proposed
acceptance contract, not an implemented size guarantee or measured speedup.
